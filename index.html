Technical depth, people first.

Engineer by training, strategist and leader by practice.

I came to Canada from India for a master's in engineering. I'll be honest: I didn't understand much of what I was learning at first. Python, MATLAB, C++, it was a lot. I still graduated with an 80+ average, and that taught me what matters most in any career: you can learn hard things from zero if you keep showing up.

I then spent five years building an analytics career in Toronto, moved to the US on a work visa, and became a Canadian citizen along the way. I'm a proud Indian who has now worked in three countries.

Today I lead data and AI value strategy at a global consulting firm. My role changes every few months. I've been a project manager, a scrum master, a product and data strategist, and a hands-on analytics implementer, working across industries from retail to financial services.

It was never always colorful. There was office politics, and I had to learn who to work with.

I'm still learning from the leaders around me, and I'm becoming the leader I wish I'd had. These sessions are my way of being that person for you.
